Our background

A South African construction company with a clear purpose.

Pulo Innovation (Pty) Ltd is a 100% black-owned General Building, Civil and Engineering company operating in South Africa, established in 2017.

The company was established in response to growing opportunities in the construction industry. It aims to contribute to empowerment through job creation, skills development and skills transfer while maintaining a sustainable business.

Led by Managing Director Atang Moahloli (Ex-Eskom Quantity Surveyor), Pulo Innovation employs skilled and experienced personnel to provide innovative, efficient and cost-effective building, solar and civil services, while maintaining high ethical and professional standards.

Leadership

Hands-On Management & Technical Expertise.

At Pulo Innovation, we believe that strong leadership drives successful projects. Our Managing Director, Atang Moahloli, brings a wealth of experience from both the public and private sectors, ensuring that every project is executed with technical precision and financial accountability.

His background as an Ex-Eskom Quantity Surveyor and Project Manager means he understands the stringent safety, quality, and compliance standards required for large-scale power infrastructure and civil projects.

Under his direct supervision, our teams deliver on-site management excellence—from quality control of reinforced concrete to strict safety compliance and program delivery.
