Who we are

Construction built on quality, integrity and experience.

Pulo Innovation (Pty) Ltd is a 100% black-owned South African general building, civil and engineering company established in 2017.

Led by Managing Director Atang Moahloli (Ex-Eskom Quantity Surveyor), we bring together skilled people, practical experience and a commitment to efficient, cost-effective service delivery. Our focus is on understanding each client's requirements and delivering work to a high professional standard.
